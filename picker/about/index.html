How to use

Draw a winner in three steps.

Upload your giveaway's comments, choose who can enter, and spin the slot machine.

  1. Upload your comments

    Drop in one or more CSV files, such as a Facebook and an Instagram export, or paste a plain list of names. Columns are matched automatically and you can remap any of them. Only a name column is required.

  2. Set the rules

    • Entries: give each person one entry, or count every comment as a ticket.
    • Filters: choose platforms and posts, comments or replies, skip empty or sticker-only comments, require keywords, or set a minimum number of @mentions.
    • Exclusions: list names or profile links to leave out, such as staff, page admins and past winners.
    • Draw settings: set the title, number of winners, reveal style, spin length, sound, and whether one person can win twice.

  3. Spin and reveal

    Press Spin or Space. Each winner gets a full-screen reveal with Check profile and Disqualify & redraw. Copy the winners list or download it as a CSV when you're done. Press F for presentation mode if you're recording or streaming.

Your data stays in your browser

Your files are read on your device and are never uploaded. Your data, rules and winners are saved in this browser so a refresh won't lose them. Start over clears everything.

Our giveaway data is open source too

We publish the comment CSV files from our giveaways alongside the picker's code, so anyone can see who entered and run the same list through the picker.
